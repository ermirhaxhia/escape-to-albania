About me

Hi, I’m your guide.

I grew up in Albania and I still get excited by a good road trip. This is the job I always wanted.

My story

Showing Albania the way I would show a friend.

I started guiding after years of watching big groups rush through places I love. Twenty people, a flag, forty minutes. It always felt wrong.

So I made the opposite: days for a small group, in my own car, with room to stop at a roadside fruit stand or spend two extra hours on a beach nobody told you about.

I’ll tell you the real history, the family stories and the best place for coffee. I’ll also tell you when to skip something.

A day with me

What a typical day feels like.

Every route is different, but the rhythm stays the same.

  1. 08:00

    Pickup at your door

    Coffee in hand, plan in my head. I’ll check how you slept and what you feel like doing.

  2. 10:30

    The first big stop

    The castle, the canyon or the old town, before the tour buses arrive.

  3. 13:30

    A long, slow lunch

    Always at a family place and always more food than you planned for.

  4. 16:00

    Swim, walk or wander

    The part of the day that depends on you: the river, the beach or the back streets.

  5. 19:00

    Back to your hotel

    Tired in a good way, with a phone full of photos and a list for next time.
