Good to know

Questions, answered.

How many people can join?

One to four guests. That’s the whole idea: a private day, never a crowd.

What is included in the price?

Private transport, fuel, your guide and the entrance fees named on each tour. Lunch is included on most days and clearly marked.

Can we change the route?

Yes. Each day is a starting point. Tell me what to add or skip and I’ll adjust the plan and price.

What if the weather is bad?

I watch the forecast for you. If the plan doesn’t work, we switch days or choose a better route at no extra cost.

How do I pay?

A small deposit holds the date, and the balance is paid on the day by cash or card.
